# Weekly Bonus — Guide

Weekly Bonus is GoldenAura's recurring activity reward in `Your Bonus Hub`. From `config/bonus_hub.php`: badge `Every Week`, `Catch weekly bonuses based on your activity`. From `config/rewards.php` `weekly`: `Make a minimum wager of $150 to unlock the bonus. Available bonus amount is updated daily at 00:00 UTC`. Educational guide, no backend here — panel shows your amount.

## Schedule

- Calculated on a weekly basis.
- Period: Sunday to Saturday.
- Pending amount refreshes daily at 00:00 UTC, final amount claimable after Saturday close.
- Check Bonus Hub — source of truth for your account. Separate from Monthly (1st to last day) and Cashback (twice weekly).

## Eligibility

- Casino bets: Slots, GoldenAura Originals.
- Live casino: from Gold (VIP) rank, Evolution / Pragmatic Play / Ezugi games only.
- Minimum $150 qualifying wager in the week to unlock. Rank affects payout split, not the game list.

## How to claim

1. Sign in at https://goldenaura.cloud (18+).
2. Open Bonus Hub / Rewards (`Your Bonus Hub`).
3. Select `Weekly Bonus` → `Details`.
4. If available, use claim action in the panel. Current wording: https://goldenaura.cloud/page/promotions
5. Claiming also grants a Rakeback Boost (+50% base rate, auto-activated, 1 hour).

## Example

Player wagers $220 on Slots Sun–Sat, above the $150 minimum. Panel updates daily, then shows $12 after close (illustrative — panel decides rate). Claim with Rank 0–29: $6 to balance, $6 to calendar over 3 days ($2/day). VIP: 100% to balance. Claim also activates 50% Rakeback Boost for 1 hour.

## Terms (summary, official page prevails)

- $150+ weekly qualifying wager required.
- Distribution: Rank 0–29 — 50% balance, 50% calendar for 3 days; VIP — 100% balance; minimum $0.01/day to calendar, remainder to balance.
- Only listed games count. Bonus Hub / https://goldenaura.cloud/page/promotions prevails. 18+. Play responsibly.

# Monthly Bonus — Guide

Monthly Bonus is GoldenAura's long-cycle activity reward in `Your Bonus Hub`. From `config/bonus_hub.php`: badge `Every Month`, `Receive monthly bonuses based on your activity`. From `config/rewards.php` `monthly`: `The bonus calculation is based on monthly activity and lost coins`. Educational guide, no backend here — panel shows your amount.

## Schedule

- Calculated on a monthly basis.
- Period: first to last day of the month.
- Activity and lost coins accrue through the month, claimable after month-end. Check Bonus Hub — source of truth. Separate from Weekly (Sunday–Saturday) and Cashback (twice weekly).

## Eligibility

- Casino bets: Slots, GoldenAura Originals.
- Live casino: from Gold (VIP) rank, Evolution / Pragmatic Play / Ezugi games only.
- Weighting uses monthly activity + lost coins; exact amount is per-account live value. Rank affects payout split.

## How to claim

1. Sign in at https://goldenaura.cloud (18+).
2. Play qualifying games during the calendar month.
3. Open Bonus Hub / Rewards (`Your Bonus Hub`).
4. Select `Monthly Bonus` → `Details`.
5. If available after month-end, use claim action. Current wording: https://goldenaura.cloud/page/promotions
6. Claiming also grants a Rakeback Boost (+50% base rate, auto-activated, 1 hour).

## Example

Player plays Slots through September 1–30. After close, panel shows $45 (illustrative — activity + lost coins decide). Claim with Rank 0–29: $22.50 to balance, $22.50 to calendar over 3 days ($7.50/day). VIP: 100% to balance. Claim also activates 50% Rakeback Boost for 1 hour. October starts at zero.

## Terms (summary, official page prevails)

- Strict monthly window; no carry-over.
- Only listed games count.
- Distribution: Rank 0–29 — 50% balance, 50% calendar for 3 days; VIP — 100% balance; minimum $0.01/day to calendar, remainder to balance.
- Bonus Hub / https://goldenaura.cloud/page/promotions prevails. 18+. Play responsibly.

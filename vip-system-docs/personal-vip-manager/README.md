# Personal VIP Manager — Guide

Your Personal VIP Manager is GoldenAura's dedicated support contact for VIP players. From `Blog-Website/content/casino-img/`: art reference `personal_vip.png` + `vip/manager.png`. Educational guide, no backend here — assignment and contact happen on the live site.

## What a manager does

- 1-on-1 point of contact for VIP account questions (bonuses, rank, limits).
- Tailored bonus / promotion heads-up based on your play and rank.
- Guidance on VIP perks: birthday reward, 250% status, withdrawals, calendar splits.
- Invite / eligibility guidance for higher tiers (Gold, SVIP, High-Roller).
- Responsible-play point of contact — can point to limits, self-exclusion, support.

What a manager does NOT do:

- No guaranteed wins, no loss recovery, no wagering advice.
- No manual rank grants, no cash advances, no bypass of KYC / terms.
- No backend in this repo — folders + READMEs only.

## Who gets one / how to reach

1. Sign in at https://goldenaura.cloud (18+).
2. Open https://goldenaura.cloud/page/vip and check your current rank / status.
3. If eligible, manager contact appears in your account / VIP panel — source of truth.
4. Use only official site channels. Never share password / 2FA. Current wording: https://goldenaura.cloud/page/vip
5. Not seeing one yet? Keep playing qualifying games and re-check VIP page — allocation is per-account and tier-based.

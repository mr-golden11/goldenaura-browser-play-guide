# VIP Birthday Bonus — Guide

VIP Birthday Bonus is GoldenAura's once-a-year treat for VIP players. From `config/vip_club.php` `special_treats` list: `VIP Birthday Bonus` — `Celebrate your Big Day with a special B-day bonus from GoldenAura!` with icon `/minimal/img/vip/birthday.png`. From `config/bonus_hub.php` `vip_perks`: `VIP Birthday Bonus` — `Celebrate your Big Day with a special B-day bonus from GoldenAura!` with art `/minimal/img/bounce_page/vip-birthday.png`. Educational guide, no backend here — your VIP manager and VIP page decide eligibility and amount.

## Eligibility

- VIP status required: Rank 30+ unlocks VIP per `config/vip_club.php` thresholds (`vip_level` 30, `svip_level` 57). SVIP Rank 57+ also qualifies.
- Account must be fully verified with date of birth on file and 18+ status confirmed.
- Active play history matters: managers (Elizabeth, Mila, Nick, Jack) award personal treats based on activity, not automatically for every account.
- One birthday reward per player per year. No carry-over to the next year.

## How to claim

1. Sign in at https://goldenaura.cloud (18+ only).
2. Complete verification so your birth date is saved before your birthday month.
3. Open https://goldenaura.cloud/page/vip and check your rank, VIP Active state, and contact options.
4. Contact your personal VIP manager or write to vip@goldenaura.cloud around your birthday.
5. Claim from the VIP Lounge / Bonus Hub if the treat appears there. Current wording: https://goldenaura.cloud/page/promotions
6. Use the credit on Slots or GoldenAura Originals, for example [Gates of Olympus](https://goldenaura.cloud/games/gates-of-olympus).

## Example

Player reaches Rank 32, verifies DOB in March, birthday in October. In October the manager posts a $25 birthday treat (illustrative — manager decides amount). Player claims in the VIP Lounge, plays qualifying slots, and any winnings follow standard bonus terms before withdrawal. If DOB was never verified, no treat appears.

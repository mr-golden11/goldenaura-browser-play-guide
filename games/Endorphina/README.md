# Endorphina at GoldenAura — Provider Overview

Endorphina is a Prague-based slot studio known for mathematically driven video slots. Its portfolio generally centres on classic and video slot formats with fixed paylines, risk/gamble options, and bonus games, often with strong graphic themes rather than complex cascading systems.

If you are new to this provider, the most useful concepts to learn first are paytable structure, fixed-line betting, the gamble feature found in many of its games, and how free-spin triggers and multipliers interact.

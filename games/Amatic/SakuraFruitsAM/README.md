## How to Play
- Sakura Fruits is a 5 reel video slot featuring Stacked symbols, Wild substitutions and the Sakura feature.
"- Press to start a new game; press while spinning to stop the reels immediately."
- The game is played with 720 ways (fixed).

## Paytable
"- Payouts according to the paytable are made only if the specified number of symbols is of a kind."
- Wild Symbol substitutes all symbols.
- Line wins are a multiple of the line bet.

## Free Games and Gamble
- Bonusspins are played at the same bet and number of lines as the initiating game.
"- With active gamble mode the player has the chance to multiply the win; double by color, quadruple by suit."

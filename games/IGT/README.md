## A short note on RTP and volatility

Think of RTP as a distant average and volatility as temperament. Calm titles stretch a small budget with frequent small hits; bolder ones trade quiet spells for bigger peaks. The reliable number is always the one shown inside the specific game window, which can vary by edition.

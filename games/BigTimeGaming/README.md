# Big Time Gaming (BigTimeGaming) — Provider Hub on GoldenAura

Big Time Gaming, usually shortened to BTG, is a Sydney-based studio best known for inventing the Megaways mechanic — variable-height reels where the number of symbols per spin changes, producing thousands of potential ways instead of fixed paylines. Its approach has been widely licensed across the industry and is generally associated with high-volatility, feature-heavy video slots built HTML5-first for browser play.

The educational value of this hub is understanding how Megaways-style games behave: how ways are counted, what cascading or reaction wins do, how increasing multipliers and free-spin triggers usually work, and why session variance can feel sharp. Future guides here would focus on reading the paytable, checking ways, stake ranges and autoplay controls, and setting time and budget limits.

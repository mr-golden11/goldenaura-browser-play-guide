Gates of Persia is the 2014 Gamomat treasure land: amphorae, daggers, golden lions, thrones and palaces on a 5x3 board with configurable 10/20/30 lines. Every reel carries super-block symbols for full-board sequences. Three or more blue-Gate scatters open 7 free games.

## Features

- **Super-Block Reels:** Every reel stacks super blocks for full-board sequences.
- **7 Gate Free Games:** Blue-Gate trios-plus open 7 free games with blocks.
- **Queen Wild:** The queen substitutes for Persian-line completions.
- **Card-Ladder Gambles:** Double on the card or climb the risk ladder.

## How to play

1. **Choose lines and stake:** Select 10, 20 or 30 lines in the game panel.
2. **Spin Persia:** Press spin. Treasure-table matches from the left pay.
3. **Trigger the Gates:** Land 3+ blue Gates for 7 free games with blocks.
4. **Stack the blocks:** Super-block stacks build the full-board sequences.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

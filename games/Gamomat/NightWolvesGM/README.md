Night Wolves is the Gamomat moonlit hunt: wolves, moons and forest symbols on a 5x3 board with 10/20/30 configurable lines. The TOP symbol pays right-to-left as well as left. The bright full Moon opens free games with stacked wilds filling the night.

## Features

- **Moon Stacked Spins:** Full-Moon scatters open spins with stacked wilds.
- **Both-Way TOP Symbol:** The TOP symbol pays right-to-left too.
- **Wolf Pack Board:** Wolf packs run the moonlit woods.
- **Card-Ladder Gambles:** Double on the card or climb the risk ladder.

## How to play

1. **Choose lines and stake:** Select 10, 20 or 30 lines in the game panel.
2. **Spin the night:** Press spin. Night-table matches pay, TOP both ways.
3. **Trigger the Moon:** Full-Moon scatters open the free games.
4. **Stack the wilds:** Stacked wilds fill the moonlit board.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

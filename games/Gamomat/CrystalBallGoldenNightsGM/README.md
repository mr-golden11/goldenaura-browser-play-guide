1. **Choose your stake:** Set the line bet within the $0.01 to $6.00 range. Five lines stay fixed.
2. **Spin the grid:** Press spin. Line wins pay left to right; balls pay scattered anywhere.
3. **Trigger free spins:** Land 3+ crystal balls for 10 free spins with a randomly crowned expanding symbol.
4. **Watch the stamps:** Bonus symbols landing across reels stamp whole columns — stacked walls pay across all five lines.

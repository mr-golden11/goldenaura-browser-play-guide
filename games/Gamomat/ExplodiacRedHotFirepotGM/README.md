## Features

- **Bomb Base Game:** Full Explodiac engine underneath: exploding bomb wilds, 10 fixed lines, card and ladder gambles.
- **Firepot Side Bet:** Optional three-level side bet from $0.20 to $1.00 that arms the jackpot trigger on every spin.
- **Three Ovens:** The jackpot round opens three ovens in sequence — three flames advance to the Win-O-Meter climb.
- **Six Jackpot Tiers:** Climb from Iron through Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold to the Jewel jackpot worth up to 10,000x.

## How to play

1. **Choose your stake:** Set the base bet from $0.10 to $100.00, then decide whether to add a Firepot side bet level.
2. **Spin the base game:** Play Explodiac lines normally: bombs explode into surrounding wilds for the 1,000x line ceiling.
3. **Arm the jackpot:** Only spins with an active side bet can trigger the Firepot round — pick a flame level that.
4. **Open the ovens:** On a trigger, three ovens open in turn. Three flames move you to the Win-O-Meter; anything less ends.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

## RTP note

The core game is listed at 96.12% RTP with the Firepot side game at a minimum 96.05%, though Gamomat supplies alternative models per market. Always check the game information panel. RTP is a theoretical long-term measure and does not predict any individual session. Study symbols and session habits responsibly.

## Features

- **Fire-Spread Wilds:** Dragon flames spread whole-reel through free games.
- **Burning Free Games:** Free games carry the fire to the final spin.
- **Knight Adventure Board:** Knights face the beast across ten lines.
- **Card-Ladder Gambles:** Double on the card or climb the risk ladder.

## How to play

1. **Choose your stake:** Set the total bet in the game panel.
2. **Spin the fire:** Press spin. Knight-table matches from the left pay.
3. **Wake the dragon:** Flame wilds spread whole-reel on the board.
4. **Trigger the games:** Scatters open the burning free games.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

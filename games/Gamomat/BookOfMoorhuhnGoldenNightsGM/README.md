The base game is the Moorhuhn book formula on 10 fixed lines: the golden book is wild and scatter, three or more open 10 free games, and a random symbol expands across reels for the round. Pharaoh chickens, scarabs and explorers form.

## Features

- **Moorhuhn Book:** The golden book is wild and scatter, opening 10 free games with a random expanding symbol.
- **Chicken Premiums:** Pharaoh chickens headline the pays toward the documented 555x ceiling.
- **Golden Nights Chests:** Side-bet spins can open three chests in turn toward the thermometer draw.
- **Seven Tiers:** Iron-to-Super-Jewel thermometer payouts with the top award to 50,000 coins.

## How to play

1. **Choose your stake:** Set the total bet in the game panel. All 10 lines stay fixed.
2. **Spin the hunt:** Press spin. Line wins pay left to right; books pay scattered anywhere.
3. **Trigger the book round:** Land 3+ books for 10 free games with the drawn expanding symbol.
4. **Add the Nights bet:** Place the Golden Nights side bet at a tier matching the session budget.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

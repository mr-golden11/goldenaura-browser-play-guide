## How to play

1. **Choose lines and stake:** Set active lines and bet level for $0.05 to $3.00 per spin. Full lines keep every fruit live.
2. **Spin the reels:** Press spin. Three or more matching fruits on an active line from the left pay.
3. **Collect small wins:** Low variance means frequent modest hits — collect them to sustain long sessions.
4. **Gamble on colour:** Push wins through the card gamble to double on correct colours, or stop and bank.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

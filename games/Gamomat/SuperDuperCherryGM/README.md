Super Duper Cherry is the Gamomat cherry power: classic cherries on a 5x3 board with 5/10/20 configurable lines. The Extra Cherries Power converts full-line cherry hits across the board. Five cherries on one line turn all visible cherries into one higher random.

## Features

- **Extra Cherries Power:** Five-cherry lines convert all visible cherries higher.
- **Cherry Top Wins:** Converted cherry boards scale to the top.
- **RHF Twin Included:** The Red Hot Firepot edition shares this page.
- **Card-Ladder Gambles:** Double on the card or climb the risk ladder.

## How to play

1. **Choose lines and stake:** Select 5, 10 or 20 lines in the game panel.
2. **Spin the cherries:** Press spin. Cherry-table matches from the left pay.
3. **Power the cherries:** Five-cherry lines convert the visible board.
4. **Chase the top:** Converted symbols scale to the top win.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

## RTP note

Super Duper Cherry is listed at 96.10% RTP in standard Gamomat configurations (additional models on request). Always check the game information panel where you play. RTP is a theoretical long-term measure and does not predict any individual session. Study symbols and session habits responsibly.

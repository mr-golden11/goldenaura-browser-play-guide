Phantom's Mirror is a Gamomat opera slot on a 5x3 board with 10/20 configurable lines. Phantom Wilds and Mirror Scatters drive line and scatter pays left to right. Three or more Mirror scatters open 8 free games where higher symbols turn into.

## Features

- **Mirror Wilds:** Higher symbols turn Wild in free games.
- **8 Free Games:** Mirror trios-plus open 8 free games with extra Wilds.
- **Selectable Lines:** 10 or 20 lines shape stakes on 5x3.
- **Dual Gambles:** Card gamble with a risk ladder after wins.

## How to play

1. **Choose lines and stake:** Select 10 or 20 lines in the game panel.
2. **Spin the opera:** Press spin. Line wins pay left to right.
3. **Raise the mirrors:** Three-plus Mirrors open 8 free games.
4. **Turn the Wilds:** Higher symbols turn Wild in the round.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

## RTP note

Phantom's Mirror is listed at 96.12% RTP in standard Gamomat configurations (additional models on request). Always check the game information panel where you play. RTP is theoretical long-term and does not predict any session. Study symbols and session habits responsibly.

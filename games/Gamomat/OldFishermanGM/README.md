Old Fisherman is the Gamomat high-seas hunt: King Salmons and sea symbols in super blocks on a 5x3 board with 10/20 configurable lines. Block stacks fill whole boards for the high prizes. Free games open the extended hunt with renewals during play,.

## Features

- **Super-Block Salmons:** King Salmon blocks fill whole boards.
- **Extendable Free Games:** Free games renew during the hunt.
- **High-Sea Prizes:** Block stacks scale to the high prizes.
- **Card-Ladder Gambles:** Double on the card or climb the risk ladder.

## How to play

1. **Choose lines and stake:** Select 10 or 20 lines in the game panel.
2. **Spin the seas:** Press spin. Sea-table matches from the left pay.
3. **Block the salmons:** Super-block stacks fill the high-seas board.
4. **Trigger the games:** Scatters open the extendable free games.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

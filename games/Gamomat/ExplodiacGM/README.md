# Explodiac (ExplodiacGM) — Gamomat

Explodiac is a retro fruit-and-fire slot from Gamomat played on 5 reels and 3 rows with 10 fixed paylines. Bomb wilds on the first three reels explode and turn every adjacent position.

Explodiac is a 2015 Gamomat release that keeps the vintage fruit-machine skeleton — cherries, lemons, oranges, plums, grapes, watermelons and red sevens over a static fire backdrop — and bolts

## Features

- **Bomb Wilds:** Bomb wilds on reels 1 to 3 substitute for all fruits and sevens to complete line wins.
- **Surrounding Explosion:** Every bomb detonates and turns all adjacent positions wild, carpeting the grid with sevens.
- **Card Gamble:** Guess the face-down card colour after any win to double it — wrong guesses forfeit the stake.
- **Risk Ladder:** Climb the prize ladder on 50/50 steps for larger wins, or drop back down on a miss.

## How to play

1. **Choose your stake:** Set a total bet from $0.10 to $100.00. All 10 lines stay fixed with no configuration.
2. **Spin the reels:** Press spin. Three or more matching fruits or sevens on a line from the left pay.
3. **Catch the bombs:** Bombs on the first three reels go wild then explode — overlapping blasts build the biggest lines.
4. **Count wilds as sevens:** Lines with multiple wilds pay as top sevens, so blast-covered lines score like premium hits.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.

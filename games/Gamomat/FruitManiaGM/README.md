## Features

- **5 Fixed Lines:** Five fixed paylines keep staking to one decision and every spin fully readable.
- **Lucky Seven Top:** Five sevens on a line pay 1,000x — the premium every climb aims at.
- **Gamble Feature:** Card-colour doubles and ladder climbs staircase any win toward the 5,000x ceiling.
- **Bell Mid-Tier:** Liberty bells bridge the fruit base and the seven top with solid mid pays.

## How to play

1. **Choose your stake:** Set a total bet from $0.01 to $20.00. All five lines stay fixed.
2. **Spin the reels:** Press spin. Three or more matching fruits, bells or sevens on a line from the left pay.
3. **Chase the sevens:** Five sevens is the 1,000x line prize — the round every gamble climb targets.
4. **Gamble the wins:** Push line hits through card doubles or ladder steps toward the 5,000x ceiling.
5. **Check paytable:** confirm stakes, line values and rules in-game, as settings vary.
